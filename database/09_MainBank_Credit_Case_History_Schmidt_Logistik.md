## 1. Purpose and scope

Every credit decision and review for Schmidt Logistik GmbH (Hanau) since 2021, measured against the Credit Policy in force at the date of the decision: v1 (credit-policy-2022-v1, valid from 2022-01-01) before 2025-07-01, v2 (credit-policy-2025-v2) from 2025-07-01. All cases live in KreditPortal. Internal rating: 5. Total exposure (Gesamtobligo): EUR 1.8 million.

## 2. Decision of 2021-09-01: working-capital line

- Date: 2021-09-01. Policy in force: predecessor rules; the decision predates Credit Policy v1 (valid from 2022-01-01) and has since been reviewed under v1 and v2 at every annual review.
- Request, amount and purpose: EUR 0.6 million working-capital line (Kontokorrent) to finance receivables and diesel purchases.
- Analysis: collateral assignment of receivables; total exposure EUR 0.6 million, under v1 within the four-eyes limit of EUR 1.0 million.
- Decision: four-eyes decision (Markt vote Corporate Banking, Marktfolge vote Credit Analysis).
- Outcome so far: still in place, used within the limit; in 2025 the assignment was extended to the Hessenfood contract (condition 3 of EXC-REQ-2025-014).

## 3. Decision of 2025-06-30: term loan for 15 new trucks

- Date: 2025-06-30. Policy in force: Credit Policy v1 (last day of validity; v2 applies from 2025-07-01).
- Request: fleet expansion by 15 new trucks for the growing Hessenfood volume, filed by Martin Scholz on form KA-01.
- Amount and purpose: EUR 1.2 million term loan, 5 years, purchase of 15 trucks.
- Analysis against v1, based on the FY2024 statements dated 2025-04-30 (within the v1 limit of 12 months): revenue EUR 22.0 million; EBITDA EUR 2.4 million, margin 10.9%; equity ratio 19%, below the v1 minimum of 20% (first deviation); debt/EBITDA 3.1x, below the v1 maximum of 4.0x; DSCR 1.25, above the v1 minimum of 1.10. Rating 5 within the v1 range for new business (1-6). Collateral: security transfer of the trucks, lending value EUR 1.5 million; the loan equals an LTV of 80% of lending value, above the v1 maximum of 70% for machinery and vehicles and above the 60% maximum of v2 (second deviation). Customer concentration: top three customers 71% of revenue, largest customer Hessenfood AG 38%. Under v1 a risk note was required only above 40% for a single customer; the analyst included one anyway, citing the Hessenfood contract term until 2028-12-31 with annual indexation and the assignment of the Hessenfood receivables as mitigants (under v2, from the next day, risk note and sensitivity analysis would have been mandatory). Total exposure after the loan: EUR 1.8 million, above the v1 four-eyes limit of EUR 1.0 million and below the Head of Credit Analysis limit of EUR 3.0 million.
- Decision: the two deviations were handled as a policy exception on the new form EXC-REQ, ahead of v2 (v1 required no separate form). EXC-REQ-2025-014 was approved by the Head of Risk Management, Dr. Katrin Lorenz, on 2025-06-24 with four conditions: (1) equity ratio covenant of at least 20% from FY2025 on, (2) quarterly interim figures (BWA) within 30 days after quarter end, (3) assignment of receivables extended to the Hessenfood contract, (4) margin add-on of 0.5 percentage points. Explicitly not a precedent. The loan was decided by the Head of Credit Analysis, Stefan Krüger, on 2025-06-30 under the v1 limit of up to EUR 3.0 million, on the votes of Martin Scholz (Markt) and Dr. Petra Hoffmann (Marktfolge).
- Outcome so far: trucks delivered and registered as collateral by Loan Operations; instalments on schedule; the four conditions are monitored.

## 4. Annual review of 2026-06-20

- Date: 2026-06-20. Policy in force: Credit Policy v2.
- Request: annual review (Jahresüberprüfung) per the 12-month cycle; no new facility.
- Analysis against v2 and the Risk Assessment Checklist (risk-checklist-2025), based on the FY2025 statements dated 2026-05-15 (within the v2 limit of 9 months): revenue EUR 23.5 million; equity ratio 21.5%, above the 20% minimum and the covenant floor (amber band). Customer concentration unchanged (Hessenfood AG 38%, top three 71%); both checklist limits (25% and 60%) remain exceeded, so the review memo carries the risk note and the sensitivity for the loss of the largest customer required by v2. Total exposure unchanged at EUR 1.8 million.
- Decision: rating 5 confirmed in RatingTool; covenant met; facilities continued. Review memo (KV-02) with Markt vote Martin Scholz and Marktfolge vote Dr. Petra Hoffmann, countersigned by Stefan Krüger because the exposure is above the v2 four-eyes limit of EUR 1.5 million. No escalation to Risk Management: no exception, no covenant breach, no rating change.
- Outcome so far: next annual review due by 2027-06-20.
