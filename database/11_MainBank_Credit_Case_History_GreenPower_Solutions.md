## 1. Purpose and scope

The credit decisions for GreenPower Solutions GmbH (Darmstadt) in 2026. Both cases fall under Credit Policy v2 (credit-policy-2025-v2, valid from 2025-07-01); none was taken under v1. Both are project finance, which v2 and the Escalation Guidelines (escalation-2025) send to the Credit Committee above EUR 3.0 million regardless of the total exposure. Internal rating: 4. Total exposure (Gesamtobligo): EUR 4.0 million; EUR 7.5 million if the second park is approved.

## 2. Decision of 2026-03-24: project loan "Solarpark Odenwald-Süd"

- Date: 2026-03-24. Policy in force: Credit Policy v2.
- Request: financing of the solar park "Odenwald-Süd" (8 MWp) through a project company, filed by Daniel Fischer on form KA-01, with the DOC-LIST items for project finance (cash-flow model, EPC contract, offtake agreement).
- Amount and purpose: EUR 4.0 million project loan, 15 years, 24-month grace period with interest only during construction. Total project cost EUR 5.33 million, equity contribution 25% (EUR 1.33 million).
- Analysis against v2 and the Risk Assessment Checklist (risk-checklist-2025): the cash-flow model shows a DSCR of 1.35 on average and 1.22 as the minimum in any year after commissioning, above the v2 project-finance floor of 1.20 average and 1.05 minimum. Construction period 14 months, commissioning planned 2027-06, first feed-in revenue 2027-Q3; the 24-month grace period covers construction and ramp-up. A grace period above 12 months (maximum 24) needs Risk Management sign-off under v2, which Dr. Katrin Lorenz gave on 2026-03-20. Revenue is fixed by a 12-year power purchase agreement (PPA) with Stadtwerke Darmstadt; construction cost and delay risk are carried by the fixed-price EPC contract with completion guarantee. Rating 4 within the v2 range for new business (1-5). The FY2025 statements, dated 2026-04-20, were not yet available at the decision; the memo used the previous audited statements and the current interim figures (BWA), both within the v2 age limits. The FY2025 statements later confirmed an equity ratio of 31% (green), revenue EUR 9.5 million and EBITDA EUR 1.9 million. ESG questionnaire completed (exposure above EUR 2.0 million). Collateral: assignment of the PPA revenues, pledge of the project company shares, security transfer of the plant. Total exposure after the loan: EUR 4.0 million, below the EUR 5.0 million threshold for ordinary lending, but project finance above EUR 3.0 million always goes to the Credit Committee.
- Decision: Credit Committee (Kreditausschuss), chaired by Jochen Richter, on 2026-03-24, on the votes of Daniel Fischer (Markt) and Dr. Petra Hoffmann (Marktfolge) and the Risk Management sign-off of 2026-03-20. Conditions: disbursement in tranches against construction progress certified by the EPC contractor; collateral registered by Loan Operations before the first tranche; DSCR of at least 1.05 in any year after commissioning as a covenant, reported with the annual project accounts.
- Outcome so far: construction on schedule for commissioning in 2027-06; tranches disbursed by Loan Operations against the EPC progress certificates. No revenue yet.

## 3. Request of September 2026: project loan "Solarpark Wetterau" (pending)

- Date: September 2026. Policy in force: Credit Policy v2.
- Request: a second park, "Solarpark Wetterau" (6 MWp), with the same structure as Odenwald-Süd (project company, PPA, fixed-price EPC contract, tranches against progress).
- Amount and purpose: EUR 3.5 million project loan.
- Analysis status: total exposure would rise from EUR 4.0 million to EUR 7.5 million, above the EUR 5.0 million Credit Committee threshold of v2; project finance above EUR 3.0 million goes to the committee in any case. The Escalation Guidelines require the submission (KV-02 plus annex) at least 5 business days before the meeting; an urgent decision (Eilentscheidung) is not an option for a new park. The analysis repeats the v2 project-finance test (DSCR average at least 1.20, minimum at least 1.05 after commissioning), the grace period rule, the ESG questionnaire and the parent's equity ratio after a second equity contribution. The committee is expected to require a sensitivity analysis (electricity price -20%, construction delay +6 months) before it decides.
- Decision: pending. Submission of the KV-02 is planned for 2026-10-07 (early submission) for the Credit Committee meeting of 2026-10-20.
- Outcome so far: case open in KreditPortal.
