## 1. Purpose and scope

This Credit Policy (Kreditrichtlinie) governs all lending to corporate customers of MainBank AG in Corporate Banking. It applies to every new facility, every increase and every annual review of a corporate exposure. Version 3 replaces Version 2 (valid from 2025-07-01) in full on 2027-01-01. Until then Version 2 remains in force. Decisions taken before 2027-01-01 remain subject to the policy in force on the decision date.

## 2. What changes compared with Version 2

Version 3 changes three rules. Everything else is carried over from Version 2 unchanged.

1. Four-eyes approval limit: raised from EUR 1.5 million to EUR 2.0 million total exposure. The relationship manager (Markt) and the credit analyst (Marktfolge) can jointly decide cases up to EUR 2.0 million. The limit of the Head of Credit Analysis (EUR 5.0 million) and the Credit Committee threshold (above EUR 5.0 million) are unchanged.
2. Minimum debt service coverage ratio (DSCR): raised from 1.20 to 1.25 for standard corporate lending. For project finance the requirement is an average of 1.25 and at least 1.05 in any year after commissioning.
3. ESG questionnaire: mandatory for every exposure above EUR 1.0 million (previously EUR 2.0 million).

Reason for the changes: the four-eyes limit is adjusted to the higher ticket sizes in the portfolio since 2025, the DSCR floor reflects the higher interest level, and the ESG threshold follows the supervisory expectations for smaller exposures.

## 3. Approval limits by total exposure (Gesamtobligo)

Total exposure is the sum of all facilities of the customer group.

- Up to EUR 2.0 million: four-eyes decision, relationship manager plus credit analyst (Markt and Marktfolge votes in the credit memo KV-02).
- Up to EUR 5.0 million: Head of Credit Analysis.
- Above EUR 5.0 million: Credit Committee (Kreditausschuss).
- Project finance above EUR 3.0 million: always Credit Committee, regardless of total exposure.

## 4. Financial requirements (unchanged unless stated)

- Minimum equity ratio 20%.
- Minimum DSCR 1.25 (changed; project finance: average 1.25 and at least 1.05 in any year after commissioning).
- Maximum debt/EBITDA 3.5x.
- Loan-to-value (LTV) at most 60% of lending value for machinery and vehicles, 80% for real estate.
- Annual financial statements not older than 9 months at the decision date; interim figures (BWA) not older than 3 months for exposures above EUR 1.0 million.
- New business only for internal ratings 1-5 (RatingTool).
- Customer concentration: a single customer above 25% or the top three above 60% of revenue require a risk note in the credit memo and a sensitivity analysis for the loss of the largest customer.

## 5. Exceptions, ESG and grace periods

- Every deviation from this policy requires form EXC-REQ, approved by the Head of Risk Management, logged in KreditPortal. An approved exception is never a precedent.
- ESG questionnaire mandatory for exposures above EUR 1.0 million (changed).
- Grace periods above 12 months (maximum 24 months) require Risk Management sign-off.

## 6. Monitoring

Annual review every 12 months, every 6 months for ratings 6 and worse. Covenant breaches, rating 6 or worse and watchlist proposals are escalated to Risk Management per the Escalation Guidelines.
