## 1. Company

GreenPower Solutions GmbH, Darmstadt, develops, builds and operates ground-mounted solar parks in southern Hesse. Founded 2018, 32 employees. Managing director and founder: Dr. Lea Winter. Customer of MainBank AG since 2023. Relationship manager (Markt): Daniel Fischer, Corporate Banking. Credit analyst (Marktfolge): Dr. Petra Hoffmann, Credit Analysis.

## 2. Business model and special characteristics

Each park is built in a separate project company, which holds the land leases, the grid connection and the power purchase agreement (PPA) and is financed by equity from GreenPower and a project loan.

The special characteristic is long-term project financing with delayed cash flows: during construction there is no revenue, and debt service can only start after commissioning. Current project: solar park "Odenwald-Süd" (8 MWp).

| Item | Odenwald-Süd (8 MWp) |
|---|---|
| Construction period | 14 months |
| Commissioning planned | 2027-06 |
| First feed-in revenue | 2027-Q3 |
| Offtake | 12-year PPA with Stadtwerke Darmstadt |
| Construction contract | Fixed-price EPC contract with completion guarantee |
| Total cost and equity | EUR 5.33 million, equity contribution 25% (EUR 1.33 million) |

Loan tranches are disbursed against construction progress certified by the EPC contractor. A second park, "Solarpark Wetterau" (6 MWp), has been requested.
