## 1. Company

Schmidt Logistik GmbH, Hanau, is a road transport and contract logistics company with 120 employees and 95 trucks. Managed and owned by Anja Schmidt (finance, administration, customer contracts) and Peter Schmidt (fleet, dispatch, drivers). Customer of MainBank AG since 2019. Relationship manager (Markt): Martin Scholz, Corporate Banking. Credit analyst (Marktfolge): Dr. Petra Hoffmann, Credit Analysis.

## 2. Business model and special characteristics

Two business lines: regional transport, and contract logistics (warehousing, picking, delivery) under multi-year contracts. Margins are thin and depend on fleet utilisation, diesel prices and driver availability.

The special characteristic is customer concentration (Klumpenrisiko). The top three customers account for 71% of revenue:

| Customer | Share of revenue | Contract | Term |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hessenfood AG | 38% | Contract logistics, annual indexation | Until 2028-12-31 |
| Rhein-Main Baustoffe GmbH | 20% | Transport | 2-year contract |
| Taunus Pharma Logistics | 13% | Temperature-controlled transport | 2-year contract |

The Hessenfood contract is the anchor; its term and indexation are the main mitigant. The two smaller contracts carry renewal risk every two years. The shares were unchanged at the annual review of 2026-06-20.
